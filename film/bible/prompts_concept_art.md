# Bible · Prompts de concept art (ChatGPT gratuit)

**À quoi ça sert** : obtenir des *dessins de recherche* pour visualiser le style avant
de modeler. Ces images ne sont pas le film et ne garantissent pas la cohérence :
c'est le modèle 3D qui la garantira.

**Mode d'emploi** (une image par jour ou deux, le quota gratuit est limité) :
1. Ouvrez ChatGPT, nouvelle conversation.
2. Joignez **une seule photo** où la personne est bien visible de face
   (Léna : photo devant la porte · Ruben : photo devant la porte).
3. Collez le prompt (en anglais : les générateurs le comprennent mieux).
4. Enregistrez le résultat dans `film/references/concept/` et envoyez-le-moi : je vérifie
   la ressemblance avec la bible.

> ⚠️ Vos photos sont envoyées à OpenAI quand vous les joignez. C'est votre choix.

---

## Prompt 1 · Portrait de Léna (face)
```
Turn the young woman in this photo into a stylized 3D animated feature film character,
keeping her exact identity: almond-shaped very dark brown eyes, thick dark well-defined
eyebrows, soft oval face with full cheeks, straight nose with a slightly rounded tip,
full natural pink-nude lips, warm golden light skin with light freckles on the nose and
cheeks, very long straight very dark brown hair with a slightly off-center part and
face-framing strands. Slightly larger expressive eyes than real life, soft subsurface
skin, hair in soft clumps. Wearing a light grey ribbed sleeveless top and a thin red
string bracelet. Front view, head and shoulders, neutral expression with a tiny
half-smile, soft warm key light, plain warm beige background. Original character design,
no existing film character.
```

## Prompt 2 · Portrait de Ruben (face)
```
Turn the young man in this photo into a stylized 3D animated feature film character,
keeping his exact identity: long face with a defined jaw, brown eyes with slightly heavy
relaxed eyelids, thick straight dark eyebrows, long straight nose, full lips, light
stubble on the upper lip and chin, light warm skin with a slight pink undertone, dark
brown hair short on the sides with wavy volume on top swept back. Calm, teasing
expression. Slightly larger eyes than real life, soft subsurface skin. Plain thick white
t-shirt. Front view, head and shoulders, soft warm key light, plain warm beige
background. Original character design, no existing film character. No hat.
```

## Prompt 3 · Planche de rotation (à faire APRÈS avoir obtenu un portrait qui vous plaît)
Joignez le portrait obtenu (pas la photo) et écrivez :
```
Using exactly this character, create a character turnaround sheet on a plain light
background: front view, three-quarter view, side profile, back view, full body,
same scale, same outfit, same hairstyle, neutral standing pose with arms slightly
away from the body.
```

## Prompt 4 · Planche d'expressions
```
Using exactly this character, create an expression sheet with 6 head close-ups in a
2x3 grid: neutral, big laugh, teasing raised eyebrow, tender smile, surprised, duck-face
kiss pout. Same lighting and background in every panel.
```

## Si le résultat ne ressemble pas
Répondez dans la même conversation, une correction à la fois, par exemple :
`Keep everything, but make the eyebrows thicker and straighter.`
`Keep everything, but the face must be longer and narrower.`
